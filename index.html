ସ୍କ୍ରୋଲ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ, କିନ୍ତୁ ବୁଦ୍ଧି ଲଗେଇ

🎮

ମଜାଦାର ଖେଳ

ଗଣିତ ଅଭ୍ୟାସକୁ ଏକ ଖେଳ ପରି ମନୋରଞ୍ଜନ କରିଥାଏ

💡

ଫଳପ୍ରଦ ସମୟ

ଆପଣଙ୍କ ଖାଲି ସମୟକୁ ଏକ ଉତ୍ପାଦକ ଅଭ୍ୟାସରେ ପରିଣତ କରନ୍ତୁ

💻📱

ସବୁ ଡିଭାଇସ୍‌ରେ

ଡେସ୍କଟପ୍‌ରେ ଅନଲାଇନ୍ ଖେଳନ୍ତୁ କିମ୍ବା ଆଣ୍ଡ୍ରଏଡ୍ ଆପ୍ ଡାଉନଲୋଡ୍ କରନ୍ତୁ

➕➖✖️➗

ଚାରୋଟି ପ୍ରକ୍ରିୟା

ଏକ,ଦୁଇ କିମ୍ବା ତିନି ଅଙ୍କ ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ସଂଖ୍ୟା ସହିତ ମିଶାଣ, ଫେଡ଼ାଣ, ଗୁଣନ ଏବଂ ହରଣ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ କରନ୍ତୁ

🔢

ପଣିକିଆ (୨କ ରୁ ୨୫କ)

୨କ ରୁ ୨୫କ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ପଣକିଆ ଶିଖନ୍ତୁ ଓ ମନେରଖନ୍ତୁ। ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ପଣକିଆ ଏକ ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ଓ ସହଜ ଫର୍ମାଟରେ (ଯେପରିକି: ୨ × ୧ = ୨ କିମ୍ବା, ଦୁଇ କେ ଦୁଇ ) ଦିଆଯାଇଛି, ଯାହା ଶୀଘ୍ର ମନେ ରଖିବାରେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରେ ।

✍️

ଅକ୍ଷରରେ ସଂଖ୍ୟା

କେବଳ ସଂଖ୍ୟାରେ ସୀମିତ ରୁହନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ! ୧ ରୁ ୧୦୦ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ସମସ୍ତ ସଂଖ୍ୟାକୁ ଅକ୍ଷରରେ ଲେଖିବା ଶିଖନ୍ତୁ। ଏହା ଛୋଟ ପିଲା ଏବଂ ଭାଷା ଶିଖୁଥିବା ଲୋକଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଏକ ଉତ୍ତମ ସୁବିଧା ।

✍️

ଅଙ୍କନ ପ୍ୟାଡ

ଖାତା ଓ କଲମର ଆବଶ୍ୟକତା ନାହିଁ! ଆମର ଡିଜିଟାଲ୍ ସ୍ଲେଟ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଅଭ୍ୟାସ କରନ୍ତୁ, ହିସାବ କରନ୍ତୁ କିମ୍ବା ସଂଖ୍ୟା ଲେଖି ଶିଖନ୍ତୁ।

🧩

ବୁଦ୍ଧି ଖେଳ

ତର୍କ ପଜଲ୍: ତର୍କ ଶକ୍ତି ବଢ଼ାଇବା ପାଇଁ ଅସୀମିତ ପଜଲ୍ ଖେଳନ୍ତୁ । ସୁଡୋକୁ: ଏକାଗ୍ରତା ଓ ସମସ୍ୟା ସମାଧାନର ଦକ୍ଷତା ବଢ଼ାଇବା ପାଇଁ କ୍ଲାସିକ୍ ସୁଡୋକୁ ଖେଳନ୍ତୁ । ଫଳ ତୁଳନା: ଏକ ମଜାଦାର ଖେଳ ଯାହା ଆପଣଙ୍କର ଦେଖିବା ଓ ତୁଳନା କରିବାର କ୍ଷମତାକୁ ବଢ଼ାଇବ ।

🧮

ସହଜ କ୍ୟାଲକୁଲେଟର୍

ଶୀଘ୍ର ହିସାବ କରିବା ଏବଂ ଦୈନନ୍ଦିନ ବ୍ୟବହାର ପାଇଁ ଏକ ସରଳ ଓ ଉପଯୋଗୀ କ୍ୟାଲକୁଲେଟର୍।

🌍

ଏହା ୧୦ଟି ଭାରତୀୟ ଭାଷାରେ ଉପଲବ୍ଧ: ଓଡ଼ିଆ, ସଂସ୍କୃତ, ଇଂରାଜୀ, ହିନ୍ଦୀ, ତେଲୁଗୁ, ଆସାମୀ, ବଙ୍ଗଳା, ଗୁଜୁରାଟୀ, ମାଲାୟଲମ ଏବଂ ଉର୍ଦ୍ଦୁ ।

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Ganita Bingya free to play?

Yes, Ganita Bingya is completely free to play. There are no hidden costs or in-app purchases.

2. Does Ganita Bingya have ads?

No, Ganita Bingya is 100% ad-free. We are committed to providing a focused and uninterrupted learning experience, and the game will remain ad-free forever.

3. Where can I download Ganita Bingya?

You can download Ganita Bingya for free from our official sources:

  • Official Website: https://imsbg.github.io/Ganita-Bingya-App/
  • GitHub Releases: https://github.com/imsbg/Ganita-Bingya-App/releases
  • Indus Appstore: https://www.indusappstore.com/...

4. What languages are supported in Ganita Bingya?

Ganita Bingya is a multilingual app. It currently supports Odia, English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Telugu, Bengali, and Gujarati.

5. Who created the Ganita Bingya app?

Ganita Bingya is an open-source math game created and maintained by Sandeep Biswal G. As an open-source project, its source code is publicly available on GitHub.

6. Was AI involved in the development of Ganita Bingya?

Yes, AI was used as a key development tool. While the programming code was generated by an AI, the core concept, user interface (UI) design, game features, and guiding prompts were all created by Sandeep Biswal G.

7. What are the key features of the Ganita Bingya app?

Ganita Bingya is designed to make learning math and language intuitive and fun. You can:

  • Practice Core Math: Sharpen your skills in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. You can customize the difficulty by choosing one, two, or three-digit numbers.
  • Master Extended Multiplication Tables: Learn multiplication tables extensively, from 2 all the way to 25. Each table is presented in both numerals and written words, with support for your local language.
  • Use the Interactive Drawing Pad: Practice writing numbers and characters in your own language on a digital canvas.

8. Who is Sandeep Biswal G?

Sandeep Biswal G is the passionate creator behind the Ganita Bingya app. Hailing from Odisha, he is a technologist and explorer with a deep love for both nature and digital innovation.

A core part of his mission is to promote and integrate the Odia language into the world of technology, making digital tools more accessible for Odisha. You can follow his journey and explorations on his social media channels:

9. What is the story behind Ganita Bingya's launch?

The story of Ganita Bingya begins with a clear mission: to create a powerful educational tool for children to sharpen their minds through math and calculation.

With this goal of building a high-quality app, the journey to launch was a meticulous one. The official public launch of Ganita Bingya was v1.7 on August 15, 2025.

The initial concept (v1.0) was developed on July 25, 2025, but it, along with versions up to 1.6, served as internal test builds. This extensive testing was crucial because Ganita Bingya was proudly launched first and foremost in the Odia language. Creator Sandeep Biswal G dedicated significant time to research and manually integrate all the Odia text, ensuring the app was an accurate and effective learning tool for its very first users.

This commitment to accessible education continued, with version 5.0.0 later expanding support to include six additional Indian languages.

10. What is 'imsbg' and what does it stand for?

'imsbg' is the official GitHub username of Sandeep Biswal G, the creator of the Ganita Bingya app. You will often see this username associated with the app's source code, development updates, and other technical projects led by Sandeep.
