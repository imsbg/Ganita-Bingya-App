Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Ganita Bingya free to play?

Yes, Ganita Bingya is completely free to play. There are no hidden costs or in-app purchases.

2. Does Ganita Bingya have ads?

No, Ganita Bingya is 100% ad-free. We are committed to providing a focused and uninterrupted learning experience, and the game will remain ad-free forever.

3. Where can I download Ganita Bingya?

You can download Ganita Bingya for free from our official sources:

  • Official Website: https://imsbg.github.io/Ganita-Bingya-App/
  • GitHub Releases: https://github.com/imsbg/Ganita-Bingya-App/releases
  • Indus Appstore: https://www.indusappstore.com/...

4. What languages are supported in Ganita Bingya?

Ganita Bingya is a multilingual app. It currently supports English, Hindi, Odia, Sanskrit, Telugu, Bengali, and Gujarati.

5. Who created the Ganita Bingya app?

Ganita Bingya is an open-source math game created and maintained by Sandeep Biswal G. As an open-source project, its source code is publicly available on GitHub.

6. Was AI involved in the development of Ganita Bingya?

Yes, AI was used as a key development tool. While the programming code was generated by an AI, the core concept, user interface (UI) design, game features, and guiding prompts were all created by Sandeep Biswal G.

7. What are the key features of the Ganita Bingya app?

Ganita Bingya is designed to make learning math and language intuitive and fun. You can:

  • Practice Core Math: Sharpen your skills in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. You can customize the difficulty by choosing one, two, or three-digit numbers.
  • Master Extended Multiplication Tables: Learn multiplication tables extensively, from 2 all the way to 25. Each table is presented in both numerals and written words, with support for your local language.
  • Use the Interactive Drawing Pad: Practice writing numbers and characters in your own language on a digital canvas.

8. Who is Sandeep Biswal G?

Sandeep Biswal G is the passionate creator behind the Ganita Bingya app. Hailing from Odisha, he is a technologist and explorer with a deep love for both nature and digital innovation.
