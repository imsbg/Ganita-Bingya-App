Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Ganita Bingya free to play?
Yes, Ganita Bingya is completely free to play. There are no hidden costs or in-app purchases.
2. Does Ganita Bingya have ads?
No, Ganita Bingya is 100% ad-free. We are committed to providing a focused learning experience.
3. Where can I download Ganita Bingya?
You can download it from:
• GitHub Releases
• Indus Appstore
4. What languages are supported?
Currently supports: Odia, English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Assamese, Malayalam, and Urdu.
5. Who created the app?
Ganita Bingya is an open-source math game created and maintained by Sandeep Biswal G.
6. Was AI involved in development?
Yes, AI was used as a coding tool, while the concept, UI, and features were designed by Sandeep Biswal G.
7. What are the key features?
Core Math (Add, Sub, Mul, Div), Multiplication Tables (2-25), Drawing Pad, Logic Puzzles, and Multilingual support.
8. Who is Sandeep Biswal G?
He is a technologist from Odisha passionate about integrating the Odia language into technology.
9. What is the launch story?
Launched on Aug 15, 2025 (v1.7), originally developed to help children sharpen math skills in their native language.
10. What is 'imsbg'?
'imsbg' is the official GitHub username of the creator, Sandeep Biswal G.
