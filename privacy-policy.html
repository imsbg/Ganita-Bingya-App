Introduction

"Ganita Bingya" is not just an app, it is a philosophy. In a world where most of our free time is spent on digital scrolling, we wanted to offer an alternative - a productive one. It is our attempt to make mathematics education simple, beautiful, and accessible to everyone.

Instead of pointlessly scrolling on social media, practice many exercises like addition and subtraction in 'Ganita Bingya'. Turn your free time into a fun and productive habit.

Our Mission

Our core mission is to remove the fear of mathematics from children's minds and help them establish a positive and joyful relationship with numbers.

Who is this for?

  • Students who want to strengthen their basic math skills
  • Parents who want to teach math to their children in a fun way
  • Anyone who wants to enjoy addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and multiplication tables, or wants to further improve their calculation skills

Our Philosophy

  • Simplicity: A clean and straightforward design that allows the user to focus on learning without any distractions
  • Fun Learning: The learning process should be entertaining like a game, not a compulsion
  • Accessible to All: This app is completely free, works offline, and will always remain ad-free

What Data Do We Collect?

Short Answer: Almost nothing.

We have designed 'Ganita Bingya' to work offline and in a privacy-friendly manner. We do not ask users to register, sign in, or provide personal information to use the core app. The app does not collect any personally identifiable data, telemetry, or analytics.

This app only stores lightweight local data on the user's device when necessary (for example, local progress, high scores, settings). This data is stored locally on the device only and is not sent to our servers.

Ads, Tracking & Permissions

'Ganita Bingya' is ad-free and will always remain so. We do not install any ad SDKs or trackers in the core app. If you download the Android APK, the app may request the minimum permissions required by your Android device to install and run. We do not ask for any unnecessary permissions.
